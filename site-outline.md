## 1. The Actual Work

Build the internal mechanism that turns distributed AI ambition into governed, adopted, measurable workflow change across Ontra.

This is not primarily an AI-strategy presentation role. The work is to create a centralized AI operations capability that can:

- convert departmental opportunities into a coherent roadmap;
- distinguish useful workflow change from AI theater;
- create practical training and enablement;
- build a cross-functional community of practice;
- clarify tool, data, security, procurement, legal, and human-authority boundaries;
- prove value in real work; and
- convert successful interventions into reusable company capability.

## 2. Source-Grounded Company Tension

**Hypothesis for discovery:** Ontra sells AI-powered workflow transformation externally while this role must make AI-native work repeatable internally—without producing tool sprawl, unmanaged risk, disconnected experiments, or governance drag.

The operating contradictions to make visible:

1. **Central roadmap vs. departmental ownership** — AI operations must create coherence without removing judgment from the people who own the work.
2. **Speed vs. trust** — teams need practical wins quickly, but tool, data, legal, security, and human-review boundaries must remain explicit.
3. **Experimentation vs. reuse** — one-off pilots generate activity; reusable patterns generate enterprise capability.
4. **Enablement vs. dependence** — a centralized team should increase local capability, not become a permanent bottleneck.
5. **Executive ambition vs. frontline adoption** — progress is real only when workflows, decisions, and behavior change.

## 3. Candidate Thesis

# Make every AI win reusable.

The role is not to collect use cases. It is to turn successful workflow changes into governed operating precedents that other teams can trust, adapt, and adopt.

Supporting proposition:

> AI adoption compounds when every successful intervention leaves behind a safer workflow, a stronger operator, clearer decision rights, and a reusable pattern.

## 4. Signature Operating Artifact — AI Operating Precedent

A role-specific record that converts an AI opportunity into a reusable operating pattern.

Each precedent resolves six questions:

1. **Work** — What specific workflow, handoff, decision, or friction changes?
2. **Value** — Which measurable operating outcome matters?
3. **Authority** — What judgment remains human, and who owns the decision?
4. **Boundary** — Which data, tools, vendors, permissions, and controls apply?
5. **Adoption** — Who owns the changed behavior, training, and reinforcement?
6. **Evidence** — What proves the intervention should scale, change, pause, or stop?

Qualitative dispositions, with visible assumptions rather than invented scores:

- Explore
- Bound a pilot
- Operationalize
- Standardize for reuse
- Repair
- Retire

## 6. Meaningful Interaction

A keyboard-accessible scenario selector changes one consequential workflow situation at a time. Illustrative scenarios may include:

- a recurring finance review;
- employee onboarding or internal knowledge access;
- sales or account research;
- customer-support preparation; and
- operational reporting or decision follow-up.

Every selection updates all six precedent fields, disposition, ownership, evidence requirement, explanatory copy, geometry, active state, ARIA state, and motion as one transaction.

Scenarios remain explicitly illustrative until validated through Ontra discovery. No scenario will be presented as an existing internal Ontra process.

## 7. Role-Derived Motion

**One-shot sequence:** Fragmented opportunity signals become a resolved operating precedent.

1. Workflow signals appear as incomplete fragments.
2. Value, authority, boundary, adoption, and evidence conditions resolve.
3. The full precedent locks into a stable readable state.
4. Reusable elements become visible across adjacent department contexts.
5. Motion settles; the visitor can replay deliberately.

No perpetual orbit, control-room sweep, dispatch queue, flywheel, or decorative loop.

**Reduced motion:** Render the complete resolved precedent immediately, preserve every label and control, and remove timed travel or pulsing.

**Mobile semantic equivalent:** A portrait precedent record with an explicit formation sequence, not a compressed desktop diagram.

### Section I — 120-day contribution plan

**Days 1–30 — Read the work**  
Map real workflow friction, active tools, stakeholders, decision rights, current experiments, governance constraints, and adoption signals.

**Days 31–60 — Establish useful boundaries**  
Create opportunity intake, intervention patterns, practical guidance, initial training, community cadence, and explicit escalation paths with accountable partners.

**Days 61–90 — Prove working precedents**  
Run bounded cross-functional pilots, instrument outcomes, capture user feedback, and document what should scale, change, stop, or remain local.

**Days 91–120 — Institutionalize learning**  
Publish reusable precedents, establish the operating scorecard and review cadence, strengthen local champions, rationalize tool/vendor decisions, and present the next roadmap based on evidence.
