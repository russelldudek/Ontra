
# Ontra - Director, AI Operations role intelligence

## Nominal role
Director, AI Operations, reporting to the VP of Business Operations & Strategy.

## Actual work
Turn fragmented company-wide AI demand into a governed portfolio of adopted workflow changes, then convert successful interventions into reusable capability without removing accountable judgment from the departments that own the work.

## Public company moment
Ontra describes itself as an AI-native private-markets platform company. Public sources report more than 1,000 customers and more than two million contracts processed. The company launched additional AI-powered products in 2025-2026 and achieved ISO 42001 certification for responsible AI management in June 2026. The role is therefore not an AI-awareness job; it is an internal operating-capability job inside a company whose product, culture, security posture, and market position already depend on AI.

## Operating tensions
1. Central roadmap vs departmental ownership.
2. Speed to proof vs security, trust, and responsible use.
3. Company enablement vs a central bottleneck.
4. Local workflow utility vs enterprise reuse.
5. Tool innovation vs vendor and stack coherence.

## Decision architecture
The role should make it easier to decide what enters the roadmap, which intervention fits the work, who owns the outcome, what human authority remains explicit, which data/tool/vendor boundaries apply, what evidence permits scale, and what should remain local, change, pause, or stop.

## Stakeholder topology
VP Business Operations & Strategy; executive sponsors; department leaders and workflow owners; embedded AI advocates; Engineering; Security; Procurement; Legal; People; Finance; Sales; Customer teams; vendors and model/tool providers.

## Success measures
Business outcome; workflow effort or cycle time; retained adoption; user confidence and capability; quality, risk, and trust; tool/vendor coherence; time to bounded proof; appropriate reuse; learning speed.

## Change burden
Departments must baseline work, name owners, practice new behaviors, retain human authority, return feedback, and participate in evidence reviews. Central AI Operations must make that participation useful and lightweight.

## Candidate thesis
Turn each AI intervention into a precedent the next team can trust.

## Original operating artifact
AI Operating Precedent: Work + Value + Human authority + Boundary + Adoption + Evidence, resolving qualitatively to Local experiment, Bounded practice, Reusable precedent, Repair, or Retire.

## Evidence selection
Vape-Jet for current hands-on AI-first operating transformation; DudeWorth for AI readiness and agentic workflow design; Amazon for launch, cadence, visibility, standard work, and scale; Compunetics for technical translation, quality systems, controls, and high-reliability execution.
