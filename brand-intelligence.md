The current official site and public Ontra graphics use a restrained black/white system with editorial cream and bright suite accents. Values below are source-sampled approximations, not claimed published brand-guide standards.

## Typography decision
Ontra's current public work combines high-contrast editorial serif headlines with clean, compact sans-serif interface copy. The campaign uses Avenir Next/Segoe UI as broadly available sans-serif substitutes and expresses Ontra's public typographic character through high-contrast scale, compact interface copy, disciplined rules, and editorial spacing. No proprietary Ontra font files are copied or claimed.

## Graphic and interface cues
- Strong black/white editorial contrast.
- Serif-led, high-impact headlines with concise sans-serif support.
- Color-coded operational categories rather than generic gradients.
- Thin rules, structured information, crisp diagrams, and substantial white space.
- Current product graphics express Negotiation, Compliance, and Governance through orange, cyan, and yellow.

## Role translation
The campaign uses an original `AI Operating Precedent` as the role metaphor. It borrows Ontra's document intelligence, precedent awareness, and action-oriented clarity without copying product UI or implying access to internal systems. Six clauses - Work, Value, Human authority, Boundary, Adoption, and Evidence - assemble into a reusable operating decision.

## Surface use
- Website: fullest editorial brand expression and interactive precedent.
- Resume and cover letter: restrained wordmark, serif/sans hierarchy, black/white base, small cyan/orange accents.
- Interview brief and 120-day plan: same rules, typography, and precedent vocabulary.
