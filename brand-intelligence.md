# Ontra Brand Intelligence

This independent candidate vision uses Ontra’s supplied horizontal wordmark as nominative company identification. The current system is intentionally editorial rather than dashboard-like: warm paper, a dark operating field, and restrained teal, coral, lilac, and gold accents used to clarify hierarchy and causal work.

The hero, mobile composition, AI Capability Charter, evidence trail, and application documents are candidate-original. Typography uses Avenir Next when available, with Segoe UI Variable, Segoe UI, Helvetica Neue, and Arial as structural substitutes.

The logo is rendered from the supplied source asset with an accessible text fallback. The candidate identity remains visibly distinct, and every public surface states that the work is independent and is not produced by or endorsed by Ontra.
