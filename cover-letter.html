My Amazon and Compunetics experience adds the operating discipline behind those mechanisms: launch cadence, visibility, standard work, escalation, quality systems, root cause, technical translation, and improvement in high-volume or high-consequence environments. I have learned that a useful AI intervention is not complete when the demo works. It is complete when the work is owned, boundaries are explicit, users are capable, evidence is visible, and the next team knows what was learned.

The independent candidate vision at https://russelldudek.github.io/Ontra/ makes that thinking concrete through an AI Capability Charter and a 120-day contribution plan. The charter shows how I would connect the work, its accountable owner, operating boundaries, and proof before deciding whether a pattern should remain local or become reusable company capability.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss where internal AI demand is already strongest, what the current mechanism protects, and where a centralized AI Operations function can create leverage without becoming a bottleneck.
