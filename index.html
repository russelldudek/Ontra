Ontra’s public mission is to transform unstructured information into insight, action, and automation.

This role applies the same discipline internally: move from scattered AI activity to repeatable operating capability.

01 / The actual work

AI strategy is not the scarce resource. Operating translation is.

The posting asks for a centralized AI operations function that owns the automation roadmap, company-wide enablement, a community of practice, governance, and the internal tool stack. The hiring signal is equally clear: hands-on execution and cross-functional programs—not AI theater.

Centralize

Create coherence without becoming a bottleneck.

One roadmap, common decision rules, visible dependencies, and clear escalation—while workflow owners retain judgment.

Enable

Build capability, not dependence.

Training, advocates, reusable patterns, office hours, and examples should make departments increasingly self-sufficient.

Govern

Make trust part of the workflow.

Security, Legal, Procurement, Engineering, data boundaries, vendor posture, and human review are designed in.

Compound

Turn proof into organizational memory.

Every successful change should produce evidence, training, controls, and a reusable precedent.
