Ontra's public product language emphasizes converting unstructured information into insight, action, and automation. The internal role must create comparable discipline across company workflows without turning the central function into a bottleneck.

Duplicated experimentation, inconsistent controls, weak adoption, vendor proliferation, and lost institutional learning - not because teams lack initiative, but because each win has no durable path into shared practice.

Bounded practice

Prepare recurring operating review inputs with AI assistance while finance retains interpretation, adjustment, and final communication authority.

01 · Work

02 · Value

Reduce manual preparation and expose unresolved variance sooner.

03 · Human authority

Finance owns interpretation, adjustments, and the final narrative.

04 · Boundary

Approved sources only; no autonomous posting or external disclosure.

05 · Adoption

Named finance owner, review checklist, examples, and feedback cadence.

Completeness, preparation effort, rework, review confidence, and retained use. Bounded proof; retain review and capture corrections, effort, and adoption.

A 120-day path from demand to precedent.

Begin with tactical empathy, install useful boundaries, prove working cases, and then institutionalize learning.

Days 1-30

Read the work

  • Map demand, workflows, experiments, tools, stakeholders, and constraints.
  • Observe where human judgment and risk already live.
Output: opportunity and boundary map.

Days 31-60

Establish useful boundaries

  • Create intake, decision rights, intervention patterns, training, and escalation.
  • Launch a working Community of Practice around active cases.
Output: operating charter and first bounded cases.

Days 61-90

Prove precedents

  • Run bounded implementations and instrument outcome, quality, trust, and adoption.
  • Capture reusable core and legitimate variation.
Output: reviewed operating precedents.

Days 91-120

Institutionalize learning

  • Publish precedents, strengthen advocates, rationalize tools, and shape the next roadmap.
Output: scorecard, library, and next roadmap.
