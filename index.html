Precedent established

Source-grounded company moment. Ontra transforms unstructured information into insight, action, and automation for private markets and describes itself as AI-native. The internal opportunity is to bring comparable rigor to how AI changes work across the company.

Hypothesis for discovery. The risk is not lack of AI interest. It is fragmented demand, local experiments, inconsistent enablement, tool sprawl, and governance that arrives either too late or too heavily.

01 · The actual work

Build the mechanism between AI ambition and adopted work.

The job combines obligations that are often split across strategy, transformation, learning, governance, procurement, and departmental operations. It succeeds only when those obligations reinforce one another.

Roadmap

Choose the work that deserves attention.

Convert distributed demand into an explicit portfolio of outcomes, owners, constraints, and evidence requirements.

Enablement

Make local capability stronger.

Training, resources, advocates, and a community of practice should reduce dependence on the central team.

Governance

Make safe execution easier.

Tool, data, vendor, security, legal, and human-authority decisions belong inside workflow design.

Central coherenceDepartment ownership

Shared mechanisms without removing outcome and judgment accountability from the people who own the work.

Speed to proofTrust by design

Bounded experiments move quickly because access, review, escalation, and evidence are explicit from the start.

ExperimentationReusable capability

The portfolio compounds only when successful work leaves behind patterns other teams can adapt.

Executive ambitionFrontline habit

Adoption is measured in changed work and retained behavior—not attendance, licenses, or announcements.

03 · Decision architecture

Make consequential choices explicit.

A centralized function earns trust by clarifying decisions, owners, and evidence—not by owning every automation.

Portfolio

What enters the roadmap?

Business importance, friction, feasibility, data readiness, adoption burden, risk, and reuse potential.

Intervention

What form should AI take?

Guidance, assistive workflow, bounded automation, agentic pattern, integration, or a deliberate no.

Authority

Who owns judgment?

Workflow owner, accountable leader, technical partner, review point, exception path, and escalation.

Scale

What happens next?

Explore, pilot, operationalize, standardize, repair, remain local, or retire.

05 · How I would contribute

A 120-day path from demand to precedent.

The entry plan begins with tactical empathy: understand the work, active experiments, systems, constraints, and existing expertise before prescribing a portfolio.

Days 1-30

Read the work

  • Map demand, workflows, experiments, tools, stakeholders, and constraints.
  • Observe where human judgment and risk already live.
  • Baseline adoption and outcome evidence.
Output: AI work map and discovery hypotheses.
Days 31-60

Install useful boundaries

  • Create intake, review criteria, intervention patterns, and escalation paths.
  • Launch practical enablement and community cadence.
  • Clarify accountable workflow ownership.
Output: operating charter and first bounded cases.
Days 61-90

Prove working cases

  • Run bounded cross-functional implementations.
  • Instrument value, quality, adoption, trust, and learning.
  • Capture what should scale, change, or stop.
Output: first reviewed operating precedents.
Days 91-120

Make learning compound

  • Publish reusable precedents and strengthen local advocates.
  • Establish portfolio review and scorecard cadence.
  • Recommend roadmap and tool/vendor decisions from evidence.
Output: next roadmap and repeatable company capability.
