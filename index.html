The actual work

Build the mechanism between ambition and adopted work.

Ontra’s product promise is to turn unstructured information into insight, action, and automation. Internally, this role must create the same clarity across departments: a visible portfolio, practical implementation support, relevant training, governed tools, and evidence of value.

The cost of leaving that tension unresolved: duplicated experiments, inconsistent controls, weak adoption, vendor sprawl, and useful learning that never compounds.

Portfolio

Choose work worth changing.

Qualify opportunities by business value, feasibility, ownership, adoption burden, boundary complexity, and learning potential.

Enablement

Make departments more capable.

Curate training, support advocates, and build a community of practice that reduces dependence on the central team.

Governance

Make safe execution easier.

Bring tool, data, security, procurement, legal, vendor, and human-authority decisions into workflow design.

The operating tension

Create coherence without confiscating judgment.

Central roadmapDepartment ownership

One portfolio view without removing accountability from workflow owners.

Speed to proofSecurity and trust

Bound experiments tightly enough to learn quickly without normalizing unmanaged use.

Company enablementCentral bottleneck

Build advocates and reusable patterns so the function creates leverage.

Local utilityEnterprise reuse

Standardize only what has earned reuse; preserve legitimate local context.

The AI capability charter

A use case becomes capability only when four things are explicit.

This is the operating discipline I would install across the roadmap, training program, community of practice, governance model, and tool stack.

01

Work

Which recurring job or decision becomes materially better?

02

Owner

Who remains accountable for judgment, exceptions, and outcomes?

03

Boundary

What may AI access, change, recommend, or never do?

04

Proof

What evidence earns continued use, repair, broader reuse, or retirement?

The transfer test

A pattern is ready to travel only when another team can use it without importing hidden context, unclear authority, or unmanaged risk.

  • The outcome improved.
  • Human authority remained clear.
  • The pattern can be reused with visible local variation.

How I would contribute

A 120-day path from demand to durable capability.

The sequence begins with tactical empathy. It earns the right to standardize by understanding current work, then installs useful boundaries, proves cases, and institutionalizes learning.

  1. Days 1–30

    Read the work

    Map demand, workflows, experiments, tools, owners, stakeholders, and constraints. Observe where judgment and risk already live.

    Output: opportunity and boundary map.
  2. Days 31–60

    Establish useful boundaries

    Create intake, decision rights, intervention patterns, training pathways, escalation, and a working Community of Practice.

    Output: charter and first bounded cases.
  3. Days 61–90

    Prove working cases

    Co-design implementations with owners and measure outcome, effort, quality, trust, adoption, and exceptions.

    Output: reviewed capability records.
  4. Days 91–120

    Institutionalize learning

    Publish patterns, strengthen advocates, rationalize tools, establish the scorecard, and shape the next roadmap.

    Output: library, scorecard, and next roadmap.
