Why the role matters now

Ontra already turns unstructured information into insight, action, and automation.

The internal opportunity is to bring comparable discipline to how AI changes work across the company - without tool sprawl, disconnected pilots, accidental policy, or a central function that becomes the bottleneck.

The operating model

Recurring finance operating review

Bounded practice

Prepare recurring operating review inputs with AI assistance while finance retains interpretation, adjustment, and final communication authority.

Gather, reconcile, and summarize recurring operating inputs before review.

02 · Value

Reduce manual preparation and expose unresolved variance sooner.

Finance owns interpretation, adjustments, and the final narrative.

Approved sources only; no autonomous posting or external disclosure.

Executive questions

Questions designed to discover where the mechanism must fit.

  1. Where is internal AI demand already strongest - and where is it least visible?
  2. Which workflows are important enough to redesign rather than merely accelerate?
  3. Where should AI Operations own the mechanism, and where must departments retain authority?
  4. What would make the Community of Practice useful rather than ceremonial?
  5. After six months, what evidence would make this capability credible?
